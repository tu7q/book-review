# Snapshot

<div class="tip tip-right">
  <small>Written by Brandon Sanderson, read by Thomas Ablett.</small>
</div>

The concept behind this short story is really cool. Basically, its possible to recreate an entire day of this one city and live through it again. It raises quesstions like can these recreated people be considered alive? And what would you do if you knew that your entire world was fake? This is the perfect tool for the local police to investigate crime. I don't really read detective or thriller novels, but the plot was engaging even if the ending was bitter sweet.
